MANUAL RUN FROM GITHUB REPO

1. Download the Repo to your device by pressing the "Code" button and Download ZIP.
2. Extract the ZIP file.
3. Navigate to the direct parent folder.
4. Open this folder in your Terminal. 
5. In your terminal, run "cd client;pnpm dev"
6. Open another terminal in the folder, and run "cd server;pnpm dev"

Running the app: 
1. Open the link: https://lockedin-bice-three.vercel.app/

HOME PAGE FEATURES: 

1. Study Spot Vibe Check
LockedIn suggests different places based on what "vibe" the user is seeking for in their study space. Toggle through the different cards
"Deep Focus", "Group Grind", "Coffee & Cram" , and "Night Owl" to see the different vibes.

Click on the "Browse all 159 spots" to view the full catalouge in the /places page. 

2. Explore by area
LockedIn also groups the study spaces near SMU by location, such as "Raffles Place", "Orchard", "Bugis", etc. 
Users can scroll and select the different location cards to view the study spots in that area.

3. Global Chat
LockedIn has a chat feature that allows users to communicate with each other about any study spots or simply chat casually. It is located in the bottom left of the screen,
under a green Globe icon.

4. Locker Bot
Locker Bot is our study spot assistant to help users find the perfect study spot for them, or to plan their next study session.

5. Save a spot
Users can save their favourite study spots by clicking the Heart icon in the top right of each study spot card. Users can view all their saved study spots in the Bookmark Icon in the top right of the Navigation Bar.


BROWSE PAGE FEATURES:
Enter the browse page by 1. Clicking the "Browse" button in the navigation bar, 2. Clicking the "Browse all 159 spots", or 3. Directly searching in the Search Bar in the Home Page. 

1. Search Bar
The browse page's search bar dynamically filters the places shown based on the user's input. 

2. Weather Display
LockedIn shows the weather in real time, based on the Weather in the central area in Singapore. 

3. Filters 
Dynamically filters places shown based on the filters toggled. 

4. Places catalouge
Shows all 159 study spots in a 2km radius around SMU.

INDIVIDUAL PLACE FEATURES: 
Each study spot has an individual page dedicated to it. Click on any study spot to view more details.

1. Place details
LockedIn shows detailed information about each study spot, including its name, address, user ratings, noise levels, opening hours, tags, and images.

2. Planning To Go
LockedIn's Planning to Go feature allows users to indicate their intent to visit a study spot today or tomorrow, and shows them how busy it is likely to be.
This data helps LockedIn increase the accuracy of the crowd levels

3. Community Insights
Community Insights allows users to view the estimated noise levels of places based on user reviews and reports. Users who are currently at the location can also indicate how busy the place is now (from Quiet to Packed).

4. User reviews
Users can view other LockedIn user reviews for the study spot. They can also create, edit, or delete their own review(s).

5. Map feature
LockedIn's integrated map allows users to visualise the study spot on a map and find the nearest route through 4 methods: Walk, Car, Bus, or Train.

PROFILE FEATURES
1. Edit PROFILE
Users can edit their profile details. (Name, Email, and Study Preferences)

2. Change Password
Users can change their password. 

3. Log out/Delete account
Users can log out or delete their account.
